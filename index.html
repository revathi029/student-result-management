How to Check Your Result

  1. Enter your Roll Number.
  2. Select your Date of Birth.
  3. Click on "View Result".
  4. Your statement of marks will be displayed.
  5. You can print your result for reference.

Important Information

  • Enter the Roll Number exactly as registered.
  • Date of Birth must match university records.
  • Results are provided for immediate information.
  • The online result is not the original marksheet.
  • Contact the Examination Cell for discrepancies.
